01

The big picture: cases and log entries

Care Cases and Household Care Log entries work together so your church can shepherd people well and never lose track of who still needs follow-up. Before you click anything, get the mental model.

Think of a Care Case as a summary of the overall situation, and a Household Care Log entry as a single interaction inside that situation. One case can hold many log entries that each record a phone call, a hospital visit, or a meal dropped off.

One case · many interactions
Care CaseThe whole situationOwned by a Care Manager who oversees the case — whether they provide care themselves or coordinate others.
Household Care LogEach interactionEvery call, visit, or act of benevolence becomes its own log entry attached to the case.

Where care happens in the system

The same care information shows up in three connected places. Knowing which door to use saves time.

🗂️

The Platform

The full record set. Create and edit Care Cases and log entries, build views, and run reports. This guide focuses here.

💛

Care Connect

The dashboard for admins and Care Managers to manage cases day to day, with KPIs and quick add.

✏️

Check-In Kiosk

Log entries can be captured on the spot as Family Notes during Check-In — handy at weekend services.

★

Remember the relationshipA Care Case answers "what is going on with this person or household?" A Household Care Log entry answers "what did we actually do, and when?" You'll use both constantly.

Depending on where you're already working in MinistryPlatform, there are three easy routes to Care Cases and the Household Care Log. Here's each one, and what you'll see when you arrive.

Click the Bookmarks icon next to your profile picture in the upper-right corner, then choose Care Connect. This opens the care team dashboard covered in Section 05.

When you're on a person's record, click the Tools button and select Create Household Care Log. As the tooltip notes, this adds a Household Care Log to a Care Case — the quick-entry tool covered in Section 06.

Care  ▸  Care Cases

04

Household Care Log records

A Household Care Log record captures a single act of care. It can stand on its own — like a quick note at Check-In — or be one of many entries tracking a larger Care Case.

Two tabs to know

Subsequent Care tab — when the entry belongs to a Care Case, this tab shows the other log records tied to that same case, so you can see the full history of care at a glance.

Primary Family tab — when a specific Contact is attached, this tab shows the other members of that household.

Standalone or part of a case

A standalone record is perfect for a one-time interaction or a Check-In note that doesn't need a full case behind it.

A case-linked record is added by the Case Manager as people provide care, building the running history of an ongoing situation.

Create a Household Care Log for a Care Case

Platform
  1. Go to Care Cases▸Care Cases.
  2. Open the Care Case.
  3. On the Care Log tab, click New Care Log Entry.
  4. Enter the details.
  5. Click Save.

Create a standalone record

Platform
  1. Go to Care Cases▸Household / Family Care Log.
  2. Click New Care Log Entry.
  3. Enter the details.
  4. Click Save.

Assign a Household Care Log

Platform
  1. Go to Care Cases▸Household / Family Care Log.
  2. Open the Household Care Log.
  3. Click Edit Record.
  4. In the Provided By field, select the Contact.
  5. Change the Care Outcome from Pending Assignment to Assigned.
  6. Click Save.
05

Care Connect: the care team's home base

Care Connect gives your care team quick access to everything they need — Care Case details, prayer requests, and needs — and lets them create and update cases and log entries without a trip to the Platform. With the right security role, this is where most day-to-day care happens.

Care Connect has two halves: the Dashboard, an at-a-glance summary of every Care Case from the past two years, and Care Case Details, a deep-dive into a single case. The logo at the top is the default image attached to your church's Domain record.

📊 Dashboard

Status tiles (KPIs), a summary of every case with color-coded urgency, and completion statistics. The first thing you see after signing in.

🔍 Care Case Details

One case in full — its log entries, prayer requests, needs, contact and household info, plus every action you can take on it.

The dashboard at a glance

KPIs are dynamic tiles that update in real time; click a status to filter the list to those cases. What you can see depends on your security role — Administrators see every case (or just their own with Show All), while Managers and Viewers see their own.

The five status KPIs

Active
Open cases with a Household Care Log entry dated in the future.
New
Start date within the past 14 days, with no future Household Care Log entries yet.
Needs Review
All entries are complete, but the case hasn't been closed. Only Care Case Administrators can review and close.
Lapsed
Still open (not marked complete) and the action date has already passed.
Closed
Closed within the last 30 days.
✦

Same numbers, two placesThese KPIs are also available as views in the Platform on the Care Cases page — handy when you'd rather work from a list than the dashboard.

Reading the colored status bars

In the Care Case Summary, a colored bar on the left of each case signals how urgently it needs attention, based on the Action Date of its Household Care Log entries.

GreenNo immediate attention needed — new or closed cases.
YellowNeeds some review — entries complete but the case isn't closed.
RedLapsed — the case is open and the care date has passed.

Each summary row also shows the household's last name and congregation, the Care Case type, the date it opened, the Case Manager, and a photo (the Contact's image, the Household's image, or a placeholder).

Statistics & finding a case

The Statistics view breaks down cases completed today, in the last 7 days, and in the last 30 days. Filter the dashboard by campus or congregation, or use the search box to find a case by name, type, or manager. If a case doesn't show up, double-check you've selected the right status category first.

Care Case Details

Click any case to open its details. The header shows the Household Name, Care Type, and Start Date; below it you'll find the case's type, dates, status, description, and Case Manager photo. Return to the list with the ✕ at the top right, or by clicking Care Connect in the menu.

How a case's status is determined

New
The Start Date is no more than 14 days old.
ActiveOpen
Has current, incomplete log entries (Action Date in the future, Completed = No) and the End Date is blank or in the future.
LapsedOpen
Has overdue, incomplete log entries (Action Date in the past, Completed = No) and the End Date is blank or in the future.
Needs ReviewOpen
All log entries are complete (Completed = Yes) and the End Date is blank or in the future. Only Care Case Administrators can review and close.
Closed
The End Date is in the past.

What you can do in Care Connect

With the appropriate security role, every core care action lives right here. Open the Menu inside a case to:

Actions from a Care Case

Care Connect
  1. Add Care Log — choose the care type and action date, and optionally a time and notes. The action time is stored in the database as Eastern time.
  2. Edit a Care Log entry — tap the pencil on an entry to update it. Editing sets Provided By to you (the authenticated user). When you edit, only the Action Date updates — the Date Provided stays as first saved.
  3. Add New Care Case — for the open household, or for anyone from the dashboard. Provide the details (see the fields below).
  4. Close Case — confirm to set the End Date and watch your statistics improve. Only Care Case Administrators can close a case.
  5. Send Message — email or text anyone in the household who has contact info on file. Messages are outbound only and are logged in the Platform; you'll get a confirmation when it sends.
  6. Call the location — on a mobile device, tap a listed location phone number to call it.

06

The Create Household Care Log Tool

This tool is the fastest way for staff and lay leaders to log a care need — and it's designed for privacy: the person logging care can record an entry without seeing other households' care entries.

Where you'll find it

Available on the Contacts, Participants / Members, and Households / Families pages. Launch it on its own, from a selection of records, or from a single open record.

What happens on save

A new care entry is created with the info you provided. If a selected contact has no household yet, the tool automatically creates one for them.

✦

Before you train your teamTo use this tool, a user must have access through their security role. A SPoC can grant access in the tool configuration. Confirm your care volunteers have it before the session.

The fields, at a glance

FieldWhat it's for
Care TypeRequiredfrom Care Cases ▸ Care Types The kind of care provided. The drop-down is populated by the Care Types list you set up earlier.
Care CaseOptional Links the entry to an existing care case associated with the household, if applicable.
Provided ByRequireddefaults to current user Who actually provided the care. Defaults to you, but you can pick another contact from the list.
Contact The contact record associated with the selected household members.
Action Datedefaults to today The date the care should be accomplished. Click the calendar icon to change it.
Completeddefaults to No Indicates whether the care has been provided. Check it if the care is already done.
NotesOptional Any notes related to this care entry — context, next steps, or prayer needs.

Step by step

Tool
  1. Go to the relevant page: Contacts, Participants / Members, or Households / Families.
  2. Open the record, or select records, if needed.
  3. Click Tools, then Create Household Care Log.
  4. If you didn't open or select records first, search for the household you're logging care for.
  5. In Care Type, select the type of care provided. These values come from Care Cases▸Care Types
  6. In Care Case, select an associated care case, if applicable.
  7. In Provided By, select who provided the care. It defaults to you — change it with the drop-down if needed.
  8. In Contact, select the contact record associated with the selected household members.
  9. In Action Date, set the date the care should be completed. Defaults to today; click the calendar icon to change it.
  10. If the care has already been provided, select the Completed checkbox.
  11. In Notes, add any notes related to this entry.
  12. Click Save. A success message displays.
  13. Close the tool.
✓

Where the entry landsYou'll find your new entry under Care Cases▸Household / Family Care Log. If the contact had no household, MinistryPlatform creates one using the last name from the contact record.

Quick reference

Keep this within reach during the session — a glossary of terms and the navigation paths you'll use most.

Terms to know

  • Care CaseThe summary of an overall situation for a person or household, owned by a Care Manager.
  • Household Care LogA record of a single care interaction — a call, visit, or act of benevolence.
  • Care ManagerThe contact who oversees a case, whether providing care or coordinating others.
  • Care Case TypeThe category of the situation (e.g., Physical Injury, New Mother Care).
  • Care TypeThe category of the action taken (e.g., Phone Call, Hospital Visit).
  • Care OutcomeThe status of a log entry (e.g., Pending Assignment, Assigned).
  • Care ConnectThe dashboard where admins and managers run care day to day.
