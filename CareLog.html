01

Start here

A Household Care Log entry is a short record of one act of care: a phone call, a hospital visit, a meal dropped off, a prayer in the lobby. Logging it takes about a minute, and it means no one in the household falls through the cracks.

📝

What it is

One entry = one interaction with one household. Who you cared for, what you did, when, and a short note.

⏱️

When to log

As soon as you can after the call or visit, while the details are fresh. Same day is ideal.

🧾

What to have ready

The person's name, what kind of care you gave, the date, and one or two sentences about what happened and what's next.

Care Case vs. Care Log entry

You'll see both words in the Platform. Here's the difference in one line each.

Care Case the whole situation
"The Smiths are recovering after Aaron's surgery." Opened once, managed by a Care Manager, closed when it's over. You usually don't create these.
Care Log entry one interaction
"Called Aaron on Tuesday to check on recovery." Many of these can belong to one case. This is what you'll be entering.
★

The one ruleIf you did it, log it. A short entry is far better than no entry. Don't wait until you have time to write it up perfectly.

i

Entered is not the same as doneCare can be scheduled in advance. You can log a visit for next Tuesday today, and the care team will see it coming. Entering it doesn't mark it done. When the care has actually happened, open the entry, tick Completed, and set the Care Outcome to closed. That's what tells everyone the care was provided.

02

Pick your route

There are three ways to enter a Care Log. They all end up in the same place. Pick the one that matches where you're starting from.

Create Log Entry Route A · most common
You know the person's name. Start from their record and use the Create Household Care Log tool. Fastest, and you can't see anyone else's care entries by accident. Go to Section 03 →
Update Existing Case Route B
A Care Case already exists for the situation and you want to add to its history. Open the case and add the entry from its Care Log tab. Go to Section 04 →
One-time Interaction Route C · standalone
A one-time interaction that doesn't belong to any case (a quick lobby prayer, a one-off call). Add it from the person's Household Care tab. Go to Section 05 →
i

Not sure which?Use Route A. The tool lets you pick an existing Care Case if there is one, and leaves it blank if there isn't. It covers almost every situation.

03

Log care from a person's record

Route A. This is the everyday way to log care. You open the person, click one tool, fill in a short form, and save.

A

Where the tool lives

Open any Contact, then click Tools near the top of the record and choose Create Household Care Log. The tooltip reads "Use this Tool to add Household Care Logs to a Care Case."

People  ▸  open a Contact  ▸  Tools  ▸  Create Household Care Log

What the form looks like

The tool opens a window with seven fields. Only two are required (marked with a red star). The household search bar sits across the top; the Notes box is on the right.

The Create Household Care Log tool  ·  required fields are marked *

Step by step

Tool
  1. Click People in the left-hand menu, then Contacts.
  2. Use the search box to find the person and click their name to open their record. Tip: searching by last name usually finds them fastest.
  3. Click Tools near the top of the record.
  4. Choose Create Household Care Log. The form opens with the person's household already in the Search bar at the top. If the search bar is empty, type the family's last name and pick the household from the list.
  5. In Care Type, pick what you did (Phone Call, Hospital Visit, and so on). Required.
  6. In Care Case, pick the case if one exists for this situation. If the list is empty or nothing fits, leave it blank.
  7. Check Provided By. It fills in with your name. Change it only if you're logging on behalf of someone else.
  8. In Contact, pick the person you actually cared for. It's usually already filled in.
  9. In Action Date, leave today's date if the care already happened today. If it happened on another day, or is scheduled for a future day, click the calendar icon and pick that date.
  10. If the care is already done, tick the Completed box. If it's planned for later, leave it unticked and come back to tick it once the care has happened.
  11. In Notes, write one to three short sentences. See Section 07 for what to include.
  12. Click Save. You'll see a success message.
  13. Click Close. You're done.
✓

One more thing for proper trackingThe tool doesn't show the Care Outcome field. Once the care is done, open your entry under Care ▸ Household Care Log, click Edit Record, make sure Completed is ticked, and set Care Outcome to the closed option. See Section 08.

✦

Logging the same care for several people?On the Contacts list, tick the box beside each person first, then click Tools ▸ Create Household Care Log. The tool will work from your selection.

04

Add an entry to a Care Case

Route B. Use this when you're already looking at a Care Case, or when a Care Manager has asked you to add to a specific case's history.

Step by step

Platform
  1. Click Care in the left-hand menu, then Care Cases.
  2. Find the case in the list (use the search box) and click it to open.
  3. Click the Care Log tab on the case.
  4. Click New Care Log Entry.
  5. Fill in the same fields as the tool: Care Type, Provided By, Contact, Action Date, and Notes. The Care Case is already linked for you.
  6. If the care is already done, tick Completed and set Care Outcome to the closed option. If it's scheduled for later, leave both as they are and come back when it's done.
  7. Click Save. The entry now appears in the case's Care Log tab.
i

Can't find the case?The list may be filtered to one view (for example, "Current Care Cases"). Try changing the view drop-down at the top of the list, or use Route A instead and pick the case from the drop-down in the tool.

05

Add a standalone entry

Route C. A standalone entry isn't tied to any Care Case. It's right for a one-time interaction that doesn't need an ongoing case behind it. You add it from the person's own record.

Step by step

Platform
  1. Click People in the left-hand menu, then Contacts.
  2. Use the search box to find the person and click their name to open their record. Tip: searching by last name usually finds them fastest.
  3. Click the Household Care tab, next to the General and Family tabs at the top of the record.
  4. Click New Care Log Entry.
  5. Fill in Care Type, Provided By, Contact, Action Date, and Notes. The household is already selected for you. Leave Care Case blank.
  6. If the care is already done, tick Completed and set Care Outcome to the closed option. If it's scheduled for later, leave both as they are and come back when it's done.
  7. Click Save. Your entry is now in the Household Care Log list.
✦

When in doubt, link itIf the household already has an open Care Case about the same situation, add your entry to that case (Route A or B) instead. It keeps the whole story in one place for the Care Manager.

06

The fields, one by one

Every route uses the same handful of fields. Here's what each one means and what to put in it.

FieldWhat to put in it
Care TypeRequired What you did. Pick the closest match from the list (Phone Call, Hospital Visit, Home Visit, Meal, Prayer, and so on). If nothing fits exactly, pick the nearest one and explain in Notes.
Care CaseOptional The ongoing situation this care is part of. Pick it if one exists. Leave blank if there isn't one. Don't create a new case just to fill this in.
Provided ByRequireddefaults to you The person who actually gave the care. It's filled in with your name automatically. Change it only when you're entering a log for someone else.
Contact The specific person in the household you cared for. If you visited the whole family, pick the main person you spoke with.
Action Datedefaults to today When the care happened, or when it's supposed to happen. Click the calendar icon to change it. If you're logging something from last week, set it to last week's date.
Completeddefaults to unticked Tick it when the care has actually been provided. Leave it unticked if the care is scheduled for a future date, then come back and tick it once it's done. Most entries you log after the fact should be ticked.
Care OutcomePlatform record only The status of the entry. When the care has been provided, set it to the closed option so the entry stops showing as open. This field isn't in the tool; you'll see it when you open the entry in the Platform or add one from a Care Case or the Household Care tab.
NotesOptional, but please fill it in One to three sentences: what happened, how they're doing, and what should happen next. See the next section.
✓

Why Completed and Care Outcome matterAn entry with a past Action Date that isn't ticked Completed shows up as overdue on the care team's dashboard and turns the case red. An entry whose Care Outcome is still open looks like care that's still waiting to happen. When the care is done, do both: tick Completed and set Care Outcome to closed.

07

Writing a good note

Your note is what the next person reads before they call or visit. Keep it short, factual, and useful. Three questions cover it.

1

What happened?

The interaction in a sentence. "Visited at Tampa General after surgery."

2

How are they?

A quick read on the person or family. "In good spirits, tired. Wife is handling the kids alone this week."

3

What's next?

The follow-up and who owns it. "Family would like meals Tue and Thu. Deanna to coordinate."

Before and after

Too thin

"Called. All good."

The next person has nothing to go on.

Just right

"Called to check in after surgery. Recovering well, back to work Monday. Family would like a meal Thursday. Pastor Dana to follow up."

What happened, how they are, what's next, and who owns it.

✦

Keep it kind and keep it safeWrite as if the family might read it one day. Stick to what you saw and what was said. Leave out medical details beyond what's needed for care, financial specifics, and anything you were told in confidence that the care team doesn't need to act on.

08

After you save

Need to check your entry, mark it done, or fix a typo? Everything you logged lives in one list.

Find your entry

Platform
  1. Click Care in the left-hand menu, then Household Care Log.
  2. Use the search box to find the household name, or sort by date to see the newest entries at the top.
  3. Click the entry to open it.

Mark an entry as done, or fix a mistake

Platform
  1. Open the entry (see above).
  2. Click Edit Record.
  3. If the care has been provided, tick Completed and set Care Outcome to the closed option. Both are needed for proper tracking.
  4. Fix anything else: correct the Action Date, update the Notes, or choose a different Contact.
  5. Click Save. The entry updates right away.

Logged it for the wrong person?

Open the entry, click Edit Record, and change the Contact (and Household, if needed). Don't create a second entry; that leaves a stray record on the wrong family.

Need to add a follow-up?

Don't edit the old entry. Log a new entry for the new interaction. Each call or visit gets its own line so the history stays honest.

09

Common questions

The questions occasional users ask most. If yours isn't here, ask the Pastoral Department.

I don't see "Create Household Care Log" under Tools.
Your login doesn't have the tool turned on yet. Contact the MinistryPlatform admin and ask for access to the Create Household Care Log tool. In the meantime, use Route C.
The person isn't in the Platform.
They need a Contact record first. Ask the office to add them, then log the care. If a contact has no household yet, the tool creates one automatically when you save.
The Care Case drop-down is empty.
There's no open case for that household. That's fine. Leave it blank and save. If you think a case should exist, tell the Care Manager rather than creating one yourself.
I'm not sure which Care Type to choose.
Pick the closest match and say what you actually did in the Notes. A slightly imperfect type with a clear note is better than no entry.
Do I need to use Care Connect?
No. Care Connect is the dashboard for Care Managers and admins. Everything in this guide works from the regular Platform.
Why can't I see other people's care entries?
That's by design. The tool lets you log care without seeing other households' entries, which protects everyone's privacy.
I made the entry but forgot to tick Completed.
Open it from Care ▸ Household Care Log, click Edit Record, tick Completed, set Care Outcome to closed, and save. See Section 08.
I logged care that happens next week. Is that okay?
Yes. Care can be scheduled in advance. Set the Action Date to next week and leave Completed unticked. When you've done it, open the entry, tick Completed, and set Care Outcome to closed.
10

One-page cheat sheet

Everything above, boiled down. This is the part to keep next to your keyboard.

Log care in five steps

Tool
  1. Open the person: People ▸ Contacts ▸ search ▸ click their name.
  2. Click Tools ▸ Create Household Care Log.
  3. Pick the Care Type. Pick the Care Case if there is one.
  4. Set the Action Date, tick Completed if it's done, and write a short Note (what happened, how they are, what's next).
  5. Click Save, then Close. Once the care is done, open the entry in the Platform and set Care Outcome to closed.

Terms to know

  • Household Care LogOne record of one act of care. What you enter.
  • Care CaseThe ongoing situation a log entry can belong to. Managed by a Care Manager.
  • Care TypeWhat you did (Phone Call, Hospital Visit, Meal).
  • Provided ByWho gave the care. Defaults to you.
  • Action DateWhen the care happened or is scheduled to happen.
  • CompletedTick it when the care has actually been provided.
  • Care OutcomeThe entry's status. Set it to closed once the care is done.
  • Care ManagerThe staff member who oversees a Care Case.
